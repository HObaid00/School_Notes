For simplicity, we can "absorb" it by prepending a 1 to the feature vector $x$ and respectively adding $\omega_0$ to the weight vector $\mathbb{\omega}$:
$$
\Large
\tilde{x} = (1, x_1, \dots, x_D)^T \qquad
\tilde{w} = (w_0, w_1, \dots, w_D)^T
$$
The function $f_\omega$ can then completely be written as 
$$\Large
f_w(x) = \tilde{w}^T \tilde{x}
$$
