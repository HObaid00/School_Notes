# Probability Space

## Components

- Sample space: $\Omega$
- Events: $\mathcal{F} \subseteq 2^\Omega$
- Probability measure: $P$

## Axioms

$$\Large
P(A) \geq 0
$$

$$\Large
P(\Omega) = 1
$$

$$\Large
P\left(\bigcup_i A_i\right) = \sum_i P(A_i) \quad \text{(disjoint)}
$$

## Key Properties

$$\Large
A \subseteq B \Rightarrow P(A) \leq P(B)
$$

$$\Large
P(A^c) = 1 - P(A)
$$

$$\Large
P(A \cup B) \leq P(A) + P(B)
$$
