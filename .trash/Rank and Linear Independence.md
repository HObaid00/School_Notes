# Linear Independence

Vectors are independent if:
$$\Large
x_n \neq \sum_{i=1}^{n-1} \alpha_i x_i
$$

---

# Rank

$$\Large
\text{rank}(A) = \text{\# of independent columns}
$$

## Properties
- $\text{rank}(A) \leq \min(m,n)$
- $\text{rank}(A) = \text{rank}(A^T)$
- $\text{rank}(AB) \leq \min(\text{rank}(A), \text{rank}(B))$
