## Definition
Electric charge is a fundamental property of matter that causes it to experience a force in an electric field.

## Unit
- Coulomb (C)

## Relation to Current
q(t) = q(t₀) + ∫ i(t) dt

## Fundamental Charge
e = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C
