## Linear Independence and Basis

Span:

$$\Large  
\text{span}(S) = \left\{ v \in V \mid v = \sum_{i=1}^k \alpha_i v_i \right\}  
$$

Linear independence:

$$\Large  
\sum_{i=1}^k \alpha_i v_i = 0 \Rightarrow \alpha_i = 0 ;\forall i  
$$

A basis $B = {v_1, \dots, v_n}$:

- linearly independent
    
- spans $V$
