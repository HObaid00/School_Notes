# Regression Problem

#### Given 
* observations $$\Large X= \{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n \}, \quad x_i \in \mathbb{R}^D $$
* targets 
  $$\Large
  y = \{y_1, \dots, y_N\}, \quad y_i \in \mathbb{R}
  $$

#### Find 
Mapping $f(\cdot)$ from inputs to targets
$$\Large
y_i \approx f(x_i)
$$
