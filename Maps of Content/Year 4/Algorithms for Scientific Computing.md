# Exercises
For all exercises in *Algorithm for Scientific Computing*, a character code (typically given after the title of the exercise) to classify different types of exercises:

**(U)** marks case studies, use cases, or exercises that illustrate further aspects of the lecture topics;

**(E)** marks exercises that have appeared in the same or similar form in previous exams (and thus illustrate how exercises in the exams could look like);

**(H)** marks exercises that will not be (or at least not fully) covered in the turtorials, but for which we provide solutions -here, the recommendation is to "try this at home"!

**(C)** marks programming examples, in which we will look at concrete implementations of algorithms on scientific computing.
