# Infrastructure-Based Wireless Networks

An **infrastructure-based wireless network** uses base stations or access points connected to a backbone network.

Examples include:

- Cellular networks such as GSM, UMTS, LTE, and 5G
- Wi-Fi networks with access points

In this model, mobile devices communicate with the infrastructure, and the infrastructure relays traffic.

Advantages:

- Easier coordination
- Central management
- Better integration with wider networks
- Support for mobility through handover

Disadvantage: The infrastructure must exist and must be reachable.
