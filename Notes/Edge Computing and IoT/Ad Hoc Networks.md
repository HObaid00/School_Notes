# Ad Hoc Networks

An **ad hoc network** is built without fixed infrastructure. Nodes communicate directly and may forward traffic for each other.

This is useful when:

- No infrastructure exists.
- Devices must self-organize.
- The network is temporary.
- Nodes are deployed in remote or emergency environments.

Examples:

- Disaster recovery networks
- Local smart-light networks
- Car-to-car communication
- Factory-floor automation

The challenge is that there may be no central authority for routing, security, or coordination.
