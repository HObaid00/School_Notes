# IoT Application Examples

IoT applications exist in many domains.

Examples:

- **Environmental monitoring**: measuring temperature, humidity, permafrost, or pollution.
- **Precision agriculture**: measuring soil, humidity, sunlight, and water conditions.
- **Smart homes**: connecting lights, thermostats, and appliances.
- **Smart cities**: parking, traffic, lighting, and infrastructure monitoring.
- **Industrial IoT**: factory automation, logistics, and predictive maintenance.
- **Connected vehicles**: sensors, maps, cameras, and vehicle-to-X communication.

The common pattern is that physical measurements become data-driven decisions.
