# Related Research Areas

IoT overlaps with several research areas.

**Embedded computing** focuses on computation inside technical systems, often with control tasks.

**Sensor networks** focus on many small networked devices, often designed for long lifetime and low energy usage.

**Pervasive and ubiquitous computing** studies computing embedded into everyday environments.

**RFID and NFC** focus on short-distance identification and interaction.

IoT is broad because it combines these traditions into Internet-connected physical systems.
