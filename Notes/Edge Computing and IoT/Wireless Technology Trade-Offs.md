# Wireless Technology Trade-Offs

Wireless technologies differ in range, throughput, cost, topology, and power consumption.

Typical trade-off:

$$\Large
\text{higher throughput and range} \Rightarrow \text{usually higher power consumption}
$$

Examples:

- Wi-Fi has high throughput but higher power usage.
- Bluetooth Low Energy targets low-power personal-area communication.
- IEEE 802.15.4 targets low-rate, low-power mesh or star networks.
- LoRaWAN targets long range and very low data rates.

Choosing a wireless technology is an architectural decision, not just a hardware detail.
