# RFID and NFC

**RFID** means Radio Frequency Identification. It is used to identify objects wirelessly, often with passive tags.

A passive RFID tag can be powered by the reader's electromagnetic field and then transmit its identifier or stored data.

Typical RFID use cases:

- Tracking parcels
- Inventory systems
- Access cards
- Logistics

**NFC** extends RFID-like short-range communication with two-way interaction. NFC is commonly used for:

- Contactless payment
- Pairing devices
- Sharing small data items

RFID is usually identification-oriented, while NFC is more interaction-oriented.
