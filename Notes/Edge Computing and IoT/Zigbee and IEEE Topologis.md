# Zigbee and IEEE 802.15.4 Topologies

Zigbee builds on IEEE 802.15.4 and supports low-power wireless networking.

Common topologies include:

- **Star**: devices communicate through a coordinator.
- **Cluster tree**: devices form a hierarchical structure.
- **Mesh**: devices can route through multiple possible paths.

In Zigbee terminology:

- The **coordinator** starts and manages the network.
- **Routers** forward packets.
- **End devices** usually sleep often and do not route traffic.

Mesh networking improves coverage and robustness but increases routing complexity.
