# Physical Layer and MAC Layer

The **physical layer** defines how bits are transmitted over a medium, such as radio frequency, modulation, and signal strength.

The **MAC layer** controls access to the shared medium. In wireless networks, multiple devices may want to transmit at the same time, so the MAC layer decides when a device may send.

Simplified view:

$$\Large
\text{Physical layer} = \text{how bits become signals}
$$

$$\Large
\text{MAC layer} = \text{who may transmit and when}
$$

For low-power IoT, MAC design is critical because idle listening and collisions waste energy.
