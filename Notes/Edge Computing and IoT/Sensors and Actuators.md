# Sensors and Actuators

A **sensor** measures the physical world. An **actuator** changes the physical world.

Examples of sensors:

- Temperature sensor
- Humidity sensor
- Light sensor
- Accelerometer
- Gyroscope
- Microphone
- Proximity sensor
- GPS receiver

Examples of actuators:

- LED
- Motor
- Relay
- Speaker
- Valve
- Display

The general control loop is:

$$\Large
\text{sense} \rightarrow \text{compute} \rightarrow \text{actuate}
$$

Example: A thermostat senses temperature, compares it to a target value, and actuates heating or cooling.
