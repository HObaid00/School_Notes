
# Definition
The gradient is basically the derivative in vector/matrix form
$$\Large
a = [a_1, a_2, \cdots, a_n]
$$
$$\Large
f(x) = a \cdot x = [a_1x, a_2 x, \dots, a_n x] 
$$
$$\Large
\nabla f(x) = a = [a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n]
$$
---
# Gradient

$$\Large
\nabla_x f(x) =
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1} \\
\vdots \\
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_n}
\end{bmatrix}
$$

## Key Results
- $\nabla_x (b^T x) = b$
- $\nabla_x (x^T A x) = 2Ax$
