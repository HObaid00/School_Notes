# Matrix Notation

- $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ → matrix
- $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ → column vector
- $x^T$ → row vector

## Indexing
- Element: $a_{ij}$
- Column: $a_j$
- Row: $a_i^T$

## Structure
$$\Large
A =
\begin{bmatrix}
a_{11} & \cdots & a_{1n} \\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
a_{m1} & \cdots & a_{mn}
\end{bmatrix}
$$
