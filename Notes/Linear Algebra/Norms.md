# Norms

## ℓ2 Norm
$$\Large
\|x\|_2 = \sqrt{\sum x_i^2}
$$

$$\Large
\|x\|_2^2 = x^T x
$$

## ℓ1 Norm
$$\Large
\|x\|_1 = \sum |x_i|
$$

## ℓ∞ Norm
$$\Large
\|x\|_\infty = \max |x_i|
$$

## General ℓp Norm
$$\Large
\|x\|_p = \left(\sum |x_i|^p\right)^{1/p}
$$

## Matrix Norm (Frobenius)
$$\Large
\|A\|_F = \sqrt{\sum A_{ij}^2} = \sqrt{\text{tr}(A^T A)}
$$
