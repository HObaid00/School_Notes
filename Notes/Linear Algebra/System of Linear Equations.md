# System of Linear Equations

A system:
$$\Large
\begin{aligned}
4x_1 - 5x_2 &= -13 \\
-2x_1 + 3x_2 &= 9
\end{aligned}
$$

Matrix form:
$$\Large
Ax = b
$$

Where:
$$\Large
A = \begin{bmatrix}4 & -5 \\ -2 & 3\end{bmatrix}, \quad
x = \begin{bmatrix}x_1 \\ x_2\end{bmatrix}, \quad
b = \begin{bmatrix}-13 \\ 9\end{bmatrix}
$$

## Insight
- Compact representation enables algebraic manipulation
- Foundation of all linear algebra
