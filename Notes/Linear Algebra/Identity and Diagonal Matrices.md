# Identity Matrix

$$\Large
I_{ij} =
\begin{cases}
1 & i = j \\
0 & i \neq j
\end{cases}
$$

## Property
$$\Large
AI = A = IA
$$

# Diagonal Matrix

$$\Large
D = \text{diag}(d_1, \dots, d_n)
$$
