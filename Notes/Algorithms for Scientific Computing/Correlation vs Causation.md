# Correlation vs Causation

Linear regression detects correlations between variables.

But correlation does NOT imply causation.

---

# Correlation

Two variables are correlated if they change together.

Example:

- ice cream sales increase when temperature rises

---

# Causation

Causation means one variable directly influences another.

Temperature causes increased ice cream sales.

---

# Confounding Variables

Sometimes a hidden variable influences both observed variables.

Example:

- more people in pools
- higher air temperature

Pools do not cause hot weather.

Temperature affects both.

---

# Important Lesson

Regression models identify statistical relationships, not necessarily causal mechanisms.

Careful interpretation is essential.
