Data and frequencies "symmetric":
$$\Large
F_k = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=-\frac{N}{2} +1}^{\frac{N}{2}} f_n e^{-i2\pi nk/N},
\qquad 
f_n = \sum_{k= -\frac{N}{2} +1}^{\frac{N}{2}} F_k e^{i2\pi nk/N}
$$
In general:
$$\Large
F_k = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=P+1}^{P+N} f_n e^{-i2\pi nk/N},
\qquad 
f_n = \sum_{k= Q+1}^{Q+N} F_k e^{i2\pi nk/N}
$$
