$$\Large
R_{total} = ∑ R_i$$

## Parallellkoppling
$$\Large
1 / R_{total} = ∑ (1 / R_i)$$
```
o-------o-------o--...---        o---[R_T]---
	  [R_1]   [R_2]   [R_N]  =>             |
```
