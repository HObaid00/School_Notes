# Limitations of Hard-Decision Classifiers

Hard classifiers make binary decisions directly.

Example:

$$ \Large
0 \text{ or } 1
$$

without expressing uncertainty.

---

# Main Problems

## No Uncertainty

Predictions provide no confidence information.

Example:

- 51% confidence and 99% confidence produce the same output

---

## Sensitive to Noise

Noisy data can drastically affect decision boundaries.

---

## Poor Generalization

Rigid boundaries may overfit training data.

---

## Difficult Optimization

Hard threshold functions are non-differentiable.

This makes optimization difficult.

---

# Motivation

These limitations motivate probabilistic classification models.
