## Possible Variants 
(in all imaginable combinations):
*  Scaling factor $1/N$ in the IDFT instead of the DFT;
	  alternatively a factor $1/\sqrt{N}$ in DFT and IDFT.
* switched signs in the exponent of the exponential function in DFT and IDFT
* use j for imaginary unit (electrical engineering)

## Shift of indices:
* periodic data: $F_k = F_{k+N}$ 
* aliasing of frequencies: $\Large e^{-i2\pi nk/N} = e^{-i2\pi n(k±N)/N}$
