## When to use what method?
 * [[Search]]: 
	 * Problem is deterministic 
	 * Goal can be checked.
 * [[Constraint Satisfaction]]: 
	  * Solution requires certain constraint
 * [[Probabilistic Reasoning]]:
	 * Stochastic Problem
	 * Uncertain Knowledge
	 * Given Model
	 * State of agent should be estimated
 * [[Logic Agents]]:
	 * Deterministic Problem
	 * Certain Knowledge
	 * New Knowledge should be inferred ($\implies$)
 * [[Rational Decision]]:
	 * Stochastic Problem
	 * Uncertain Knowledge
	 * Best Action should be determined
 * [[Learning]]:
	 * Stochastic Problem
	 * Uncertain Knowledge
	 * Data/Simulation instead of Model
	 * Best Action should be determined
## Learning Outcomes
* You can analyze problems of artificial intelligence and judge how difficult it is to solve them.
* Recall the basics concepts of intelligent agents and know possible task environments.
* Formalize, apply and understand [[Search]] problems
* Understand difference between between [[Constraint Satisfaction]] and Classical [[Search]] Problems as well as apply and evaluate various constraint satisfaction approaches.
* Critically asses the advantage and disadvantage of [[Logics]] in Artificial Intelligence.  
* Formalize problems using [[Propositional]] and [[First-Order Logic]].
* Apply automatic reasoning techniques in [[Propositional]] and [[First-Order Logic]]
* Understand the advantage and disadvantage of probabilistic and logic-based reasoning.
* Apply and critically asses methods for probabilistic reasoning with [[Bayesian Networks]] and [[Hidden Markov Models]].
* Understand and know how to compute rational decisions.
* Have a basic understanding on how a machine learns.
* Know the basic areas and concepts in robotics.
