## Applying Law as Rules

Legal reasoning pattern:

$$\Large \text{IF conditions THEN legal consequence}$$

Example:

- IF offer AND acceptance  
- THEN contract  

---

## Legal Reasoning (Subsumption)

Steps:

1. Identify rule  
2. Identify facts  
3. Match facts to rule  
4. Derive conclusion
